# Synthetic Case Study: Citizen Request Prioritisation AI

## Scenario

This case is entirely fictional. Imagine a municipal service team considering a decision-support tool that groups incoming service requests into suggested review priorities. A staff member remains responsible for reviewing the context and deciding what action, if any, to take.

The prototype does not contain or call an AI model. The scenario below describes a hypothetical governance exercise, not a real service, agency, dataset, decision or operational process.
