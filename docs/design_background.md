# Design background and attribution

The initial prototype used NIST AI RMF's GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE functions as conceptual design inspiration and listed OECD AI Principles for background. The current operational workflow, per-risk matrix, categories and treatment references are Saudi: SDAIA-P145 v1 (April 2026), with the SDAIA AI Ethics analytical crosswalk and separately identified Saudi regulatory sources.

There is no runtime dependency on a NIST or OECD framework implementation. Neither is a mandatory requirement of this prototype. Historical references are retained here to attribute the earlier design, not as current compliance criteria:

- [NIST AI RMF](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)
- [NIST AI RMF Playbook](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/playbook/)
- [NIST AI RMF Resource Center](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/)
- [OECD AI Principles](https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/ai-principles.html)

Control IDs such as GOV-01 and TRN-01 remain project-defined identifiers, not official Saudi or international control numbers. Published source documents and code history remain the record of earlier design choices.
